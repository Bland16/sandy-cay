This is live, not a picture — click any bar. The point of the change is that the bar stops trying to hold the notes and becomes a way in to them, which is what removes the crowding: three notes on Thursday no longer means three stacked lanes, because the bar only ever has to say how many and the panel says what.

Tinting the bar is inside the spec. D-1 refuses a tint on the column — that is reserved for a real scheduling state — but it explicitly keeps tags available "for tinting its own chip". The bar is the note's chip, so this is the allowed half of that decision, not a reversal of it. Still nothing coral: a holiday is a fact, not a problem (P-1).

No action buttons in the panel — you said not to bother with blocking, and it is a model change belonging to the next item anyway. These panels state facts only. Easy to add later.

What I'd pick, and why

D″. It keeps what you liked about D — Thanksgiving reads as one continuous run across Wed–Fri, so its length is visible rather than inferred from the same word appearing three times — while fixing the two things that made D expensive:

1. Crowding is gone by construction. Each day owns exactly one segment. A day with five notes shows the first label and +4; it never grows a second lane, so a busy Thursday is the same height as a quiet Tuesday. That is the difference between a week that looks busy and a week that is busy, which is the P-1 line.

2. The weekend drawer stops being a problem. Under D, a band spanning Friday into Saturday got cut in half, because the drawer renders Sat/Sun as a separate <WeekGrid>. Under D″ there is nothing to cut — each day already draws its own segment, and the continuity is just two neighbours sharing an edge. The drawer needs no special case, which means no third copy of anything (sharp edges #14/#17).

A′ is the safe pick and is a one-line change from what already shipped. Its only real loss is that a three-day holiday still reads as three separate marks. D′ is the honest middle: a true single span, but the day with the notes on it has to carry a +N that belongs to a run of days, which reads slightly wrong on the days where those extra notes aren't.

On the panel: the right panel is the one I'd choose. It is the locked Layout B+C idiom, it costs no new vocabulary, and it has room to grow the per-note detail (source, range, and later the actions). The popover is faster to dismiss and keeps your eye on the week — but it is a fourth anchored-overlay pattern alongside the day ⋯ menu, the Clear Day panel and the occurrence menu, and it has nowhere to go on a phone.
